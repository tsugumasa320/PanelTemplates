Horizontal 1U-to-3U converter, 8HP x 3U (40.3 x 128.5 mm). Three Intellijel 1U rows, each holding up to 6HP of modules. Per row: a 30.0 x 22 mm body window with a 3.2 mm capsule slot above and below (side rails 5.15 mm / ~1HP each).

The top row's upper slot and the bottom row's lower slot sit on the 3U rail centres (Y = 3 / 125.5), so the same milled slots mount the adapter to the rack and the outer modules. No separate plated rail pads. Screw+nut.
